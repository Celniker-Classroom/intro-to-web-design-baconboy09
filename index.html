My Childhood

I was born in Palo Alto, California, in the heart of the Silicon valley! I don’t remember it much because my family moved to Nashville, TN when I was one and a half. I remember being a happy kid. I went to an independent school where I made many friends and I loved all my teachers (except for one). I was a gifted student, so school always came easy for me back then. I helped a lot of my friends learn scratch when we first began the program in third grade. I lived in the same neighborhood with my best friend, and we used to bike around the nearby lake together while we observed the wildlife. A lot of my friend group and our families traveled together for beach vacation in Florida for fall break and Memorial Day weekend. I lived in Nashville for nearly a decade before moving to California.

The Move

We moved to San Diego when I was 10, during the COVID-19 pandemic. We moved because my parents were born and raised in California and my grandparents still live here in San Diego. The move was hard for me because I left all my friends behind and it was not easy to make new ones doing online school during a quarantine. But I spent a lot of time with my family and my dog, Jasmine. And as soon as it became safe to go out, we took our first family trip since our move, and my first trip to Maui. A couple years later my aunt and uncle and cousins also moved here from Atlanta, GA, so now we have even more family here.

Now

Highschool has not been very easy for me. I still don’t have the kind of friends I had in Nashville and I am not entirely sure where I want to go to college or what I want to study there. Many of my family members are doctors. I am interested in medicine, but I also like computers, technology, and robotics, so I am also thinking about biomedical engineering.
